01 / THE IDEA

What it explores.

My aim is to build a personal desktop assistant that can listen for a wake phrase and respond without relying on a remote language-model API. I have been iterating on AI-generated scripts and testing local behavior.

HONEST CREDIT I guide the ideas and direction. AI tools generate most code and iterations. Limited manual testing is not comprehensive verification.
02 / CURRENT STATE

What exists.

  • A local Ollama language-model setup has been tested.
  • Offline speech-recognition and text-to-speech components have been explored.
  • Selected microphone and wake-phrase tests have been completed on the local Windows machine.
03 / EVIDENCE & LIMITS

What it doesn't prove.

  • This is not a public downloadable app or a finished personal assistant.
  • Limited tests do not prove long-term reliability, security or robust environmental speech detection.
  • Future system-control actions require their own safeguards.
04 / FUTURE DIRECTION

Still to do.

These are proposed next steps, not completed deliverables.

  • Continue safe wake/standby behavior and error handling.
  • Keep sensitive device actions explicitly permission-gated.
  • Broaden local repeatability and stability tests.
