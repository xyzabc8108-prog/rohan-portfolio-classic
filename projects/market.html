01 / THE IDEA

What it explores.

I wanted a place to explore market-analysis interfaces, chart patterns and research presentation. AI tools generated most implementation. The publicly available experience is intentionally limited.

HONEST CREDIT I guide the ideas and direction. AI tools generate most code and iterations. Limited manual testing is not comprehensive verification.
02 / CURRENT STATE

What exists.

  • A Render-hosted research website exists.
  • A public demo emphasizes fictional examples and safety restrictions.
  • Research-oriented interface elements and chart concepts are included in the project.
03 / EVIDENCE & LIMITS

What it doesn't prove.

  • It is not a brokerage service and does not provide public trading instructions, authenticated live signals or guaranteed forecasts.
  • Fictional demonstration values should never be mistaken for real market data.
  • Some features and third-party content require additional licensing and safety review.
