01 / THE IDEA

What it explores.

I wanted to simplify short cinematic car and bike edits. Using AI tools to implement the idea, the project explores selecting vehicle media, applying motion styles and producing short exports.

HONEST CREDIT I guide the ideas and direction. AI tools generate most code and iterations. Limited manual testing is not comprehensive verification.
02 / CURRENT STATE

What exists.

  • A password-protected web service exists in Render under the connected account.
  • Photo-based video composition and MP4 export are part of its development workflow.
  • Media upload and audio-selection options are being developed and refined.
03 / EVIDENCE & LIMITS

What it doesn't prove.

  • The linked Render website requires owner credentials; it is not an open public demo.
  • The editor is not a full multitrack professional video tool.
  • Uploaded media may be temporary and must comply with applicable media rights. Manual testing is limited.
04 / FUTURE DIRECTION

Still to do.

These are proposed next steps, not completed deliverables.

  • Improve core export reliability and reproducible tests.
  • Review image libraries and licensing controls.
  • Consider more flexible editing only after fundamentals are stable.
