01 / THE IDEA

What it explores.

My concept is to compare audio-only, video-only and combined signals for identifying manipulated media. The implementation has been primarily AI-assisted. The early experimental result is useful for learning, not a final validation.

HONEST CREDIT I guide the ideas and direction. AI tools generate most code and iterations. Limited manual testing is not comprehensive verification.
02 / CURRENT STATE

What exists.

  • An initial FakeAVCeleb v1.2 workflow prepared 941 video clips.
  • A training, validation and test split was created for the experiment.
  • One held-out experiment with 119 test clips reported 97.48% fusion accuracy. This is a preliminary dataset-specific measurement.
03 / EVIDENCE & LIMITS

What it doesn't prove.

  • The research is still underway and is not a completed thesis or published paper.
  • The reported metric is not evidence of reliable detection on other datasets or in the real world.
  • Broader testing, careful error analysis, reproducibility and final documentation are still needed.
04 / FUTURE DIRECTION

Still to do.

These are proposed next steps, not completed deliverables.

  • Review failures and evaluation design.
  • Evaluate generalization under controlled conditions.
  • Complete reproducible documentation before making stronger research claims.
